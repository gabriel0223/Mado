Tips for games
  1. No echo of your partner's voice: in Chrome's picker choose the Window tab, pick the game, and turn on the window-audio switch if Chrome shows one. That sends only the game's sound, not Discord.
  2. If you have to share the entire screen, its audio includes Discord, so your partner hears themselves. Use Mado's voice chat instead of Discord then. Mado keeps its own sounds out of the shared audio.
  3. Run the game in borderless / windowed mode. Exclusive fullscreen often shares as a black screen.
  4. Wear headphones.
  5. Laggy? Click Stats. "Limited by CPU" → choose 720p. "Limited by bandwidth" or high loss → Low bandwidth. "Relayed" means a direct link wasn't possible, so it adds delay.
